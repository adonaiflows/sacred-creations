- Business name: Sacred Creations
- What they sell / offer: Liquid Gold, a hand-crafted facial oil and currently the only product, kept that way on purpose so all the focus goes into one oil. Every bottle goes through a ritual: it's smoke-cleansed (white sage, then palo santo and/or copal, then sweet grass), hand-crafted, and then charged on the altar with crystals for at least 21 days.
